Privacy policy

Cameras is a personal app used only by its owner to view his own cameras.

When you sign in to Google (Nest), the app receives permission to list your Google Home homes and Nest devices and open their live video. It uses that permission for nothing else. That sign-in token is stored only on the device where you signed in, encrypted by that device. Nothing is sent to any server run by this app; there is none. Ring and Wyze sign-ins are kept the same way.

The app does not collect, sell, share or keep any personal data. Video is shown live. To show each camera's tile before its live video starts, the app keeps one recent still picture per camera in its cache on that device; those pictures never leave it. Clips and snapshots are saved only when you choose to save one, to your own device.

Signing out in the app deletes the sign-in token from that device. To remove the app's access at Google itself, remove "Cameras" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
