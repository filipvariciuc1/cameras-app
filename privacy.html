Privacy policy

Cameras is a personal app used only by its owner to view his own cameras.

When you sign in to Google (Nest), the app receives permission to list your Nest devices and open their live video. That sign-in token is stored only on the device where you signed in, encrypted by that device. Nothing is sent to any server run by this app; there is none.

The app does not collect, sell, share or keep any personal data. Video is shown live and saved only when you choose to save a clip or snapshot, to your own device.

To remove access, sign out in the app or remove "Cameras" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
